	control (external) events (to an extent). From this understanding, I am able to identify both my autonomous and intentional responses (e.g., how I am likely to feel, response, etc.). At this point, I am able to identify

	now with the understanding that while I am potentially unable to control (or otherwise influence) the outcome, I can control how I response, react, or otherwise choose to act based on the stiumli of said external event / situatuion / etc.


2. Sammy the Shark teaches Gordy that survival requires a proactive mindset. What does it mean to stop waiting to be fed and start feeding yourself?
	I view that mindset as a mental starting point. Understanding, but not being limited to, one's current situatuion, available resources, and (potentially more importantly) the goal of where one desires to go (e.g., goals to acheive, etc.),
	one can begin a projective analysis of "getting to there [the goal] from here [current situatuion in life]". Growing up, financial indendence and stability were two very important life-goals I had (due to external sitmuli which I was unable
	to control - e.g., life cricumstances I experienced while growing up). Because these goals eminated to my very core (I identified where I currently was), I then identifed what I wanted (desired, really) to acheive (I identified where I
	wanted to be), and, from there, I deevised a long-term plan to achieve said goal. That being said, in the situatuion of the shark and goldfish, "stop waiting to be fed," to me, is the "in-between" of where you currently are and where
	you want to be; one is not going to be idle waiting for what they desire to come to them but rather they are going to determine what action is to be taken to get to said goal and then, in the present, at least begin taking said action.

4. When faced with setbacks, how can you encourage others on your team to move forward instead of dwelling on what went wrong?
	From both experience and from reading in the interactions between Gordy & Sammy, first you acknowledge the setup happened. As in any multi-stage process which involves acceptance, acknowledging the situatuion took place is the first step.
	Once the occurance of the situatuion has been acknowledged, the next step is to determine the desired goad. For example, in the book, Gordy had to acknowledge he did not work as hard as he could have, on a given day, to find food. Once
	he accepted this fact, he determined what he wanted his outcome to be (i.e., find more food in the coming day(s)). Once he identified his goal, he worked with Sammy to identify the steps he could take to arrive at his goal: namely, not
	stop working to find food only part way through the day but, in similar fashion to Sammy, continue working through the entire day with one focus being to outwork everyone else in the ocean who is looking for food.

7. Sharks must keep swimming to survive. What is one non-negotiable daily habit you must establish to guarantee your long-term growth?
	One common early for even first daily habits of many successful individuals relates to, as one of the first things in the morning, performing either a mental or physical (e.g., hand writing) activity of identifying the goals the
	individual wishes to acheive that day. Performing a brief post-mortum from the previous day typically is a good starting point for the morning's "review session", as in any situatuion to know where someomne is going they frist
	need to know where they're either starting or otherwise coming from. At this point, identifying the goals the individual wishes to acheive for the day in question is a logical next step in said review process and, while not
	exhaustive, the next step is to identifying the "how" the individual sees to achieve that day's goals. Performing this review session (which can be doine as an individual is getting themselves going for the day - e.g., making
	one's bed, eating their first meal, etc.) helps to bring the individuals day into focus, at least makes an attempt at reducing any uncertainty associated with said day, and also helps the individual to start the day with
	a focus mindset - just to name a few items.
